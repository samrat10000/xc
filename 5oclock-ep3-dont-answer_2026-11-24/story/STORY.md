# 5 O'CLOCK · Episode 3: "Day 3: Don't Answer" (第三日・答えるな)

**Format:** 60.0 s (1,440 frames @ 24 fps), 1080×1920 vertical (9:16), 15 shots, `toon-anime` style.

**Spine:**
- **Hero:** Sam, 17 (overnight survivor inside Officer Tanaka's koban; headphones, grey hoodie, red glasses — which he must remove under Rule 5).
- **Place:** Reflected Kiriyama (all writing mirrored, left-hand traffic, 1964 radio tower, coastal bus stop `バス停`, koban `番交`).
- **Clock:** `16:59 → 17:00` (5 PM bosai musen chime).
- **Rules in play:** Rule 4 (*Don't count the people at the bus stop. There is always one more.*), Rule 5 (*Nothing red outside.*), Rule 6 (*If someone from the town waves at you, don't wave back.*), and Rule 2 (*If the speaker says your name, do not answer.*).
- **Escalation:** The sky rift tears twice as wide as Ep1/Ep2; **The Listener** rises **right behind the 1964 radio tower** (2× larger/closer) and slams a colossal arm into the rice fields. Its central rusted horn speaks Sam's name in Real Mom's voice.

**Hidden rewatch detail:** In Shot 05 (*Always One More*), while the townsfolk stare straight ahead, Grandma Hoshino gives Sam a tiny 3-degree head-shake "no" over 6 frames.
