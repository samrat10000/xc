The
Fourth Note

Every evening the town speaker plays four notes. Today it plays three. Sam feels the fourth against his teeth.

Character study

Sam, at the crossing.

A tell before he speaks

Sam listens with his chin tucked. When the chime stalls, his thumb goes to the brass fastening at his throat. That small habit gives the ending somewhere to land.

An indigo work jacket sits high over wide pleated trousers. The cuffs turn back to oxblood. In the rain, the soaked hem should drag a beat behind his wrist as he pulls.

Listen for the missing note.

Three chimes

Three notes travel down the street. Sam mouths the fourth from memory. His lips close on silence.

The line draws tight

The red speaker cable lifts out of a puddle. Sam catches it. His heel slips before his weight takes the strain.

Something listens

The cable snaps free. In the quiet, a bronze bell the size of a house tips sideways over the roofs, listening.

The fourth note

The missing note hums in Sam's closed mouth. His collar clasp chatters against his thumb. Across the tracks, the bell turns straight toward him. Cut.
