What v1 got wrong, and the fix

Review of v1

Sam
The trike drives straight through the zombie.
A real hit. The trike rams a walker at speed, the body folds over the front cage and cartwheels into a parked car. Rigid-body ragdoll physics, no slow-mo.
Sam
Zombies look funny: green toy skin, plaid shirts, blue jeans, stiff poses.
Gaunt, realistic bodies with grey-yellow mottled skin, black veins and milky eyes. Torn, dark, rain-soaked clothes. Mocap shamble and twitch, mostly backlit so their faces show only in flashes. Showing less is scarier.
Sam
Bullets lack realism: glowing lines and a glass bubble in bullet time.
Every shot gets a muzzle flash that lights the street for 1 frame, plus gun smoke, falling brass, recoil shake, a head snap, blood mist and wall sparks. Tracer on every 5th round only. Bullet time follows a real copper round through the rain.
Sam
Drift and camera angles were wrong: the trike sat still while the camera looked on.
One continuous 180° handbrake drift with the crane camera orbiting with the trike. Tyre smoke, water spray, four headshots across the arc, then a smooth stop.
Sam
The Stalker simply appears behind the truck.
It shoves the truck. The camera crash-zooms over the roof to its face (eyes, jaw, rain on bone), then pulls back fast as the truck slides at the rider. Then it charges.
Claude
Road covered in black cow-pattern puddle decals; the truck is a white box; buildings are flat boxes.
Real CC0 scanned wet asphalt with puddles masked by roughness, a rusted box truck, and buildings with real facades, signage and AC units, from Poly Haven, ambientCG and BlenderKit.
Claude
Rider face reads plastic; trike too small in wide shots.
Skin with subsurface, pores and rain beads; wet hair. Center-framed, longer-lens wides keep the trike big in frame, the Fury Road rule.
Claude
Rendered at 40% resolution with 12 samples on cloud CPUs.
Full 1080×1920 on your GPU with adaptive sampling and the OptiX denoiser. About 1,150 frames at about 4 s each is roughly 1.5 h per full pass.
