# Camera moves Sam loves (Dead Roads Ep2)

Sam (2026-11-21): "very, very cool camera angles". Reuse these in every episode.

1. **Roadkill side-follow (shot 3).** A low side camera on the impact side, 22 mm, 1.6 m high. It drifts along the road with the trike, and the aim swings from the trike nose to the flying body's hip, so the body stays centre-frame while it cartwheels onto a parked car. Shutter .25 keeps the body sharp enough to read.
2. **Truck THUD lock-off (shot 10).** A static 50 mm from behind the rider's shoulder, framing the whole truck side. The truck rocks twice with ripples in the puddles, and nothing is shown yet.
3. **Crash zoom over the roof (shot 11).** The camera starts on the rider's shoulder (32 mm), arcs UP and over the truck roof, and lands about 2.5 m from the Stalker's face. The lens goes 32 to 58 mm during the move (move plus zoom together). It holds on the eyes and jaw, then races back and up over the roof (an arc with a sine lift) to end behind and above the rider as the truck slides at them. Rule: keep the camera above roof height until the push starts, so the creature is never hidden.
4. **Inches, low at the tyre (shot 12).** A 20 mm lens almost on the asphalt (0.3 m) beside the front tyre. The truck grinds to a stop a hand's width away, then the camera cranes up and back while the antlers rise over the roof. Shallow DOF at 3.4 m, f/4.
5. **Ep1 favourite.** The reindeer/Brute leap (10/10).

Grammar: centre framing (Fury Road), one continuous move per beat, a lens change inside the move, a low road camera, and the subject kept centre-frame through fast action.

## Handheld documentary (Sam 2026-11-21: "we need more of these shots")
The opening 1-2 s of the roadkill shot reads like someone on the street filming it: an eye-height position, an operator's natural shake, nothing drone-like. Make it the DEFAULT for ground-level coverage. Recipe: a 1/f-style layered shake (0.4-1.5 Hz sway of about 2-4 cm, 6-9 Hz micro-jitter of about 3 mm, small roll of about 0.5-1 deg). The operator reacts late to action (aim lags 3-5 frames), with small reframes, and the camera stays at 1.5-1.7 m. Use drone or crane moves only for reveals.
