# Viral research notes for The 43rd Round v2 (2026-11-20)
Sources are marketing blogs, not Instagram data; treat them as heuristics.
- Ranking signals attributed to Mosseri (Jan 2025): 1) watch time, 2) likes per reach, 3) DM sends. Sends weigh most for reach to non-followers. Reels need audio, original content, no watermarks. (dev.to/stklen "3 official ranking factors")
- Hook: the first frame must read with sound off; people swipe at ~1 s and commit at ~3 s. Aim for over 60% (ok) and over 80% (strong) retention at 3 s. Start mid-action. (blitzcutai.com)
- Clarity beats fancy editing; large readable text only when it's needed, no clutter; trending audio rarely rescues a weak idea. (creatorsjet.com, 200 viral reels)
Implications for us: frame 0 must already show motion (no slow build), the "42 rounds" text stays as the only text, the ending must loop into frame 0 (dry click -> 42), and we need a "send this to a friend" moment (the unkillable Stalker reveal).
