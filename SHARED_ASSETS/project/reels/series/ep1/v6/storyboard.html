Ideas I'm adding

  • The reel is a loop. Shot 1 is the last four frames of the lunge. When it replays, the end runs straight into the start. Replays push a reel further than likes do.
  • The phone is the title card. All on-screen text comes from inside the world: the 3:12 clock, the call screen, the red BIOHAZARD alert. No titles over the beauty shots.
  • She looks back, then she's there. The Screamer stops, turns to Sam's window, vanishes, and reappears with her face pressed to his glass. Now it's personal.
  • A 15 s teaser first. Shots 2 to 7 cut as a standalone first-person scare reel, posted a day before the episode.
  • Sound tells the story first. Inside the flat everything is muffled and quiet with no score. When the door bangs open, the world gets loud. The score proper starts in the stairwell and drops at the garage shutter.
  • One planned silence. Under the overpass, music fades out completely. Only rain and the clicks. It makes the Stalker scream hit twice as hard.
  • The friend calls back. The episode ends with his phone lighting up in the dark: incoming call, his friend. Cut. That's Episode 2's hook, and it makes people follow.
  • Never a cheat. No slow-mo used twice. No blended frames. Every freeze is one take. Every cut lands on the beat.

Characters to rebuild

  • Sam (a normal human). Full finger bones and joint limits on the rig, so hands bend only like real hands. Real motion capture (free CMU library) for waking up, reaching, grabbing keys and running. Fingers checked against the phone and keys so nothing passes through. Face mostly in shadow or phone light. Hand close-ups get their own detailed hand model.
  • Brute v2. 4 m, hunched, plated spine, split jaw, one swollen mutated arm, rebar through the chest, glowing infected sores, charred grey skin. No jeans, no smooth pink skin.
  • Stalker mouth. Thick black stitches across the lips that tear one by one, stretched skin, blood and black drool strings, a red-lit throat.
  • Crawler and runners. Dirtier and wetter, with rot and blood, no clean white clothes. The Crawler's whole sound is rebuilt.
