Style and realism rules
Look
Found footage: every ground shot is handheld documentary at eye height, with operator micro-shake, late reframes, slight focus breathing and motion blur. Never drone-smooth. Three shots (4, 13, 16) are the dash camcorder itself: VHS softness, chroma bleed, head-switch noise on the bottom 8 px, a small REC ● 3:47 AM in the corner and nothing else.
Signature moves Sam loves: the low side-follow keeping the body centred (shots 3, 14), the crash zoom over a roof to the creature's face and fast pull-back (shot 9), and the 20 mm camera on the asphalt at the tyre (shots 8, 15).
No slow-mo in this episode. Ep2 already used it. Everything is real speed. The J-turn is one smooth motion with no stop-start.
Lights: the car's left headlight is dead from shot 5 on and stays dead. No flickering or toggling lights anywhere.
Humans: perfect anatomy. The pull-up in shot 6 is a forearm grip, so no fingers splay. Relaxed hands hang palm to thigh. Compare against real reference photos before sending a frame.
Realism: wet scanned asphalt, ordinary messy streets, sensor noise in the dark, lens smudge in rain, AgX grade without over-saturating. Nothing glossy or symmetric.
