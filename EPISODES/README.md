## Series in one paragraph
**The city falls at 3:12 AM. Sam builds the Night Terror car and drives to rescue three friends in three cities.** Creatures: Walker, Runner, Husk, Crawler, Screamer, Brute and the main monster, the **Stalker** (dead bull skull with burnt antlers, stilt legs, red ember eye). Style: 3D ink line "pencil sketch" look, handheld documentary camera, dash camcorder as the in-story VHS camera, real recorded music (no AI guitar), real sound effects.

## Rules that apply to every episode (highlighted)
- **Realism first:** it must never look AI-made. Perfect human anatomy, hands never splay.
- **Real sound only** before the score starts; **no sudden music cut-outs**; **one line of dialogue at a time**, score built around the lines.
- **No slideshow camera:** handheld, real angles, crash zoom, tyre cam, low side-follow.
- **Name every shot** `number_shortname` (for example `909_crash-zoom-skull`) and never overwrite a version Sam has seen.
- **Render on the home Asus GPU** (Blender 5.0.1 portable), one GPU job at a time. Never on the office laptop.
- **Send every finished shot** as its own short clip; do not wait for a batch.
