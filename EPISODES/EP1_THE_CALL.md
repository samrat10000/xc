## Story
Sam wakes to a phone call from his friend **Akash** at the moment the city falls. He gets through the corridor, the dead elevator, the stairwell and the lobby horde to the garage, gets into the Night Terror and drives out. Chase on the road, the Stalker lunges at the overpass, black. Ends on the empty seat with the door open, Akash calling, then voicemail.

## Rules learned from the rejected versions
- **No reused shots Sam asked to fix** (crawler blur, freeze skid, potato Brute, hollow Stalker mouth, headlight flicker). Keep his favourite v5 garage reveal.
- **One look only:** the ink 3D world, no mixing with photo plates or photoreal phone. VHS blur must not kill the ink lines.
- **Close the story gaps:** keys, exit, a reason for the stairs, a lobby, getting into the car, phone placed on the seat.
- **Dialogue first, then music:** one line at a time with a breath; Akash and the radio get different voices; no synthetic buzz at the start.
- Stalker skull is a **real dead bull/cow skull**, scary mouth, glowing red eye (not a flat dot).
- **Music:** orchestra plus 80s synth plus real recorded electric guitar doubled by bass (the locked v5 sound). Sam never wants the AI "ACE-Step" versions.
