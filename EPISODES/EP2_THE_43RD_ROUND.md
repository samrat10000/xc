# Episode 2: The 43rd Round (Night Terror: Dead Roads)

> **Status: v1 (30 s, 2026-11-21) rejected by Sam except the final leap. v2 plan (48 s, 17 shots, 4 acts) approved to build, in progress in the repo `dead-roads-ep2`.**

## Story
A rider on the **IVORY trike** with 42 rounds takes on a horde and an unkillable Stalker. Hook text on frame 0: **"She brought 42 rounds."** The episode ends on the **Stalker's leap**, then black. (Ep3 opens exactly on that leap.)

## Plan v2 fixes (from Sam's review)
- **The trike must really hit the zombie** (ragdoll), not pass through.
- **Scary, realistic zombies** (Mixamo mocap), real gunfire VFX, **one-take orbit drift**.
- **The Stalker shoves the truck**, with a crash zoom to its face and a fast pull-back.
- Real CC0 assets (Poly Haven, ambientCG, BlenderKit), downloaded on the Asus.
- Score: Flow "Action Horror Cue", 120 BPM, kills on beats; real slow-mo only once.
