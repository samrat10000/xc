# Creative Brief — Episode 1: "Borrowed Time"

**Concept Inspiration:** Reference Reel 2 (`@puss_art_` / `DZC5NnpIwXe`) — Dark surreal gothic room where a silhouetted short-haired girl stares up at a glowing crimson Roman-numeral clock surrounded by smaller word-clocks while entities creep out of a red-framed doorway behind her.

## How We Make It 10x Better
1. **True 3D Spatial Depth & Genuine Crimson Lighting:** Instead of flat 2D layers, build a real 3D room in Blender 5 EEVEE with a recessed 3.0 m dark hallway behind the left doorway, 3D carved clock bezel + cracked glass lens + wrought-iron hands casting real shadows onto a scratched charcoal-plaster wall, and genuine crimson point/area light illuminating the doorway edge, floorboards, and girl's hair silhouette.
2. **Living Articulated Girl Silhouette (`kid_girl.blend`):** Real rigged character (cap and backpack removed, bob hair preserved) in a natural relaxed standing pose (never straight-down arms), subtle chest breathing, swaying hair tips, and an uneasy `4°` head tilt toward the doorway during Intrusion 2. Backlit with a crisp Layer-Weight crimson rim.
3. **Visceral Doorway Intrusion 1 — Articulated Stalker (`2.0s–4.9s`):** First, gaunt void-black fingers reach out and **physically wrap around the red wooden doorframe jamb** (`f048–f065`). Then the elongated silhouette glides partway out with two crisp glowing white pinhole eyes that **blink once at `f088–f090`** before silently retracting.
4. **Sculpted Eldritch Intrusion 2 (`5.4s–7.9s`):** 5 tapered curved 3D tendrils studded with 8 recessed darting eyes (white sclera + crimson iris + black pupil) unfurl organically from the doorway void and retract completely before `f190`.
5. **10 Diegetic Word-Clocks + Hidden Rewatch Detail:** `LOST`, `FADE`, `WAIT`, `EMPTY`, `STILL`, `ALONE`, `LATE`, `OFF`, `HUSH`, `STAY` with twitching hands—plus the tilted diamond clock `ALONE` flickers to blood-red **`DON'T TURN`** during `f125–f166` while attention is pulled to the doorway tendrils.
6. **Exact `9.000 s` Seamless Loop (`216 frames @ 24 fps`):** Camera push/sway, girl's breathing, drifting crimson dust motes, clock hand angles, doorway emptiness, and orchestral/clockwork audio wrap cleanly from `f216` back to `f001`.

## Beat Sheet (`9.000 s` / `216 frames`)
- **`0.00s–1.80s` (`f001–f043`):** Locked/breathing room hook. Giant crimson clock ticks crisply forward once per second (`12:00 -> 12:02`), red glow washes over the girl silhouette & 10 word-clocks; doorway is empty.
- **`1.80s–4.90s` (`f044–f118`):** Clock hands snap into frantic **counter-clockwise** spin. At `f048`, stalker fingers wrap the red doorframe jamb; at `f065–f106`, stalker leans out with glowing white pinhole eyes and blinks once (`f088–f090`), then withdraws by `f118`.
- **`4.80s–5.40s` (`f116–f130`):** Main clock slams dead-stop onto `III:00` (3:00 AM) with a brief red flare.
- **`5.20s–7.90s` (`f125–f190`):** Diamond clock `ALONE` flickers to blood-red `DON'T TURN` (`f125–f166`). Girl tilts head `4°` slightly left (`f148–f186`). 5 sculpted multi-eyed black tendrils curl out of the doorway (`f130–f190`) and retract completely.
- **`7.90s–9.00s` (`f191–f216`):** Doorway is empty again; main clock hands execute a smooth damped return to exact `f001` positions; breathing & dust motes return cleanly to loop seam.
