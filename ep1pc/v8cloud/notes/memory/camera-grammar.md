Sam (2026-11-21) loved the Ep2 camera work. The full notes are in /mnt/project-files/reels/series/shootout/v2/camera-moves.md (the same file is CAMERA-MOVES.md in the dead-roads-ep2 repo).
- Handheld documentary is the DEFAULT for ground coverage. It looks like someone filming from the street, at eye height, with a natural operator shake and late reframes, never drone-like. Sam: "we need more of these shots."
- Signature moves to reuse:
  - A low side-follow that keeps the flying body centred.
  - A crash zoom up and over the roof to the creature's face, then a fast pull-back. Keep the camera above any occluder.
  - A 20 mm camera on the asphalt at the tyre.
- Vehicles: tyres must read as RUBBER, matte and squashed or bulging when flat, never metal.
**Why:** Sam said these angles are "crazy", the best part so far.
**How to apply:** Plan every shot in each episode with these first, and log new moves Sam praises into camera-moves.md.
