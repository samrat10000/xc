Sound plan

No background music

  • Only real recordings: rain, thunder, sirens, footsteps on the right surface, a real V8 (start, idle, rev, pass-by), tyre squeal and skid, using the teaser's credited CC0 / CC-BY set.
  • One music piece, about 6–8 s, from 135 to the black at 92.5 s, landing its hit on the Stalker's drop. Candidate: the ending of the v5 score Sam loved, re-timed and blended into the rain.

Voices

  • Sam keeps the deep male voice Sam liked, for both narration and on-screen lines. The voice is locked; the female-sounding take is gone.
  • Akash has a different male voice through the phone band.
  • Narration only goes in gaps, never over a call line.
  • Every line sits 9–12 dB above its bed, and everything else ducks under it.

Dynamics

  • The apartment is quiet (around −26 LUFS short-term), the stairs build, and the chase and the Brute are loud (−14).
  • The bridge drops to near silence before the music.
  • The master is −14 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBTP.

What changes from v7

From Sam's review

  • The Screamer scream is restored and the floating turn is gone.
  • No Sam is left on the sofa during the key grab.
  • The corridor voice is male.
  • Stairs, lobby, B1 and garage play as one continuous run.
  • No invisible hand with the phone: Sam is shown in the dashcam.
  • Car sounds are at teaser grade.
  • The Brute has an outcome: a hit, a J-turn, an escape.
  • Teaser Stalker, parked car at the bridge.
  • No music except the climax piece; narration is clear.
  • Hidden-camera documentary shots and an IG hook.

From my own review

  • No tripod wide repeated four times.
  • One car throughout (the black fastback).
  • The missing gate and street exit are built.
  • Every shot is a 1080 final from the Asus, with no 720p or cloud test frames.
  • No dead air before the ignition.
  • Two full-cut review passes before anything is called final.
