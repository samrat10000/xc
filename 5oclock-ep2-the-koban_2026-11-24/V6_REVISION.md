# Episode 2 v6 · Revision decisions

This revision addresses the independent audits of v5. [My v5 review](V5_SELF_REVIEW.md) · [Claude's v5 review](../CLAUDE_REVIEW_EP2.md)

## Picture

- Open on a glimpse of the Listener, then the mirrored alarm. Shorten the alarm, desk and phone holds. Preserve the six-frame kettle clue, the four chime attacks, the intentional seven-frame blackout and the written ending.
- Make readable Sam reactions in the street, at Tanaka's book, in the shoulder contact and during the chime. V6 source variants keep the earlier scene sources and masters available.
- Give the street a moving three-quarter angle, foot push-off, hip and chest counter-rotation, and finger curl on the brooms. Extend the side-view scenery past the camera bounds.
- Put a larger view of the canonical Listener between the third and fourth chime beats. This reuses the Episode 1 render of the series creature in an Episode 2 cutaway.
- Restore Japanese on Tanaka's old pages and keep the six rules from the Episode 2 brief. Keep the closing warning in English for readability. The story difference between the two existing rule lists is documented in V5_SELF_REVIEW.md.
- The ending note is finished earlier in the shot. A longer held read carries the final words on a vertical screen.

## Sound

- Native takes drive the cut slots. The first line is now 2.64 s and Tanaka's is 5.01 s. Sam's alarmed response starts on one shot and carries over to the next. The spoken explanation of 16:59 is dropped because the timestamp and reaction tell that beat.
- Use location-specific beds and audible mechanical impacts. The knock has its gap. Mirror-Sam has a quiet interior bed and short door reflections.
- The piano and chime use recorded instruments. Claude's series theme opens the reel and returns on the street and at the Listener.
