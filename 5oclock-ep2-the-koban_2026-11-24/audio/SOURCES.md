# Episode 2 audio sources

- Score uses original MIDI-like note timing and recorded samples from [Salamander Grand Piano v3](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano) by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0. Samples were trimmed, repitched for adjacent notes, EQ’d and mixed. Copy of the license is included beside the mixer.
- Kiriyama’s four-note chime uses recorded marimba and glockenspiel from [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/), CC0. The pitch sequence comes from the Episode 1 production project and is reused here.
- Recorded phone buzz, broom sweeps, steps, paper, kettle, hinge, door and knocks use the CC0 recordings listed in recording metadata. They were trimmed, filtered, placed and mixed from the publicly playable previews.
- The quiet outdoor bed uses [lake&river NL 03 140203_0040](https://freesound.org/people/klankbeeld/sounds/219008/) by klankbeeld, CC BY 4.0. The edit uses a low-pass, fades and excerpts.


Dialogue uses the named ElevenLabs takes from VOICE_SCRIPT.md when they are present. Sam and Mirror-Sam share a source voice. The scored cut uses recorded instruments and foley.

ElevenLabs v4 generation history records George for narration, Roger for Sam and Mirror-Sam, and Sarah for Mirror-Mom and the hum. The downloaded takes and the final placement report are in this folder.

Episode 2 v6 uses the shared **The Fourth Note** theme arranged by Claude for 5 O'CLOCK. The corrected recorded stems, cue sheet and sample credits are in [series_theme_v6/README.md](series_theme_v6/README.md). The story chime is mixed separately from the theme.
